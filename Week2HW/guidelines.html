Guidelines For Handle With Care

1. Confidentiality Matters

What is shared in our meetings, stay in our meetings. We ask that you only share your personal stories, and to not share others outside of our meetings.

We are a space for vulnerability, we protect it.

2. Questions Are Welcome, Disrespect Is Not

Curiousity is encouraged. Shaming, attacking, or dismissing others is not.

We can hold different perspectives while still honoring each person's dignity.

3. Respect Names & Pronouns

We make a good faith to use the names and pronouns people share. If mistakes happen, we correct gently and move forward without defensiveness.

Effort Matters

4. No Political Debates

Handle With Care is focused on relationships, family support, and well-being, we are not partisan politics.

We center people, not political arguments.






Handle With Care is a safe space for all, and we take the safety and care of our community seriously.
