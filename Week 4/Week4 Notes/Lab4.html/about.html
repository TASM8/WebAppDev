About DnD Companion

The goal of this website is to help those that feel overwhelmed, or wanting to start playing DnD and do not know where to begin. DnD Companion is here to guide you and give helpful tips to get you and/or your friends started!

A Fellow Adventure's Idea

The idea came when friends and family wanted to try DnD but did not know where to begin. As a neurospicy person, I like to have a place that has great resources and ideas in one place, that way, you do not have to keep looking for hours and get overwhelmed.
